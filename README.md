# **Moves & Abilities**

## **Primary Ability: Thundervolt**

Thundervolt boosts the power of the move *Thunderbolt* by 30%. Voltbolt takes a few seconds to lock its multiple tails to act as a unified acceleration chamber as it charges up to shoot its high-power energy strike. The blast turns the space touching it into plasma as it passes by. If performed with all three tails, the blast deals an additional 50% damage to any shields or barriers, as well as additional knockback.

## **Visuals & Mechanics**

Voltbolt is an electric-steel type that is covered in a dense yet surprisingly soft coat. Its fur ranges from gold to orange. The fur is constantly charged with static electricity, naturally repelling dirt, dust and debris; however, the pelt is sleek and streamlined rather than coarse.  
Voltbolt has 3 fluffy tails with hints of chrome where its primary electrical energy lies. A Voltbolt needs its tails and without them it cannot live. Each tail has the ability to fire a *Thunderbolt*, and two or more can be combined to perform a *Thundervolt*.  
Under the fur, the skin of a Voltbolt is a sort of biological carbon fibre. This allows it to absorb the violent recoil and immense heat generated whenever its tails channel a full-power *Thunderbolt* blast. The insides of a Voltbolt act as a particle accelerator, constantly providing energy to the tails and charging up for any attacks.

## **Life Cycle**

Unlike Vulpix, Voltbolt is born with all three tails but they have no attacks. Its tails gain their moves as it grows up. A Voltbolt can only successfully breed if its partner is a member of the following species: Eeevee (and all evolutions), Vulpix, Zorua, Pikachu, Fennekin, Absol, Rockruff, Ditto.

## **Habitat/Region**

Kanto, Johto, Sinnoh, Paldea.  
However, it is far less often seen in Kanto than other regions.

# **Evolution: Voltbolt2**

When Voltbolt evolves, it keeps its objective cuteness. When it evolves, Voltbolt gains two new midnight-black, bat-like wings that allow it to fly short distances. These wings can be hidden in a way that adds no additional obstruction.  
As it evolves, its thick carbon-fibre skin thickens, yet it remains just as agile.
